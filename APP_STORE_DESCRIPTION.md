## Short Description (30 characters)

## Description

**PhoTags** is a powerful photo gallery app that helps you organize and find your photos effortlessly using custom tags.

### Key Features

**📸 Smart Photo Organization**
- Browse all your device photos in a beautiful grid view
- Tag photos with custom labels for easy organization
- Filter photos instantly by selecting one or multiple tags
- Find exactly what you're looking for in seconds

**🏷️ Flexible Tagging System**
- Create unlimited custom tags for your photos
- Apply multiple tags to a single photo
- Edit or delete tags with a simple long-press
- Tags are automatically synced across all your photos

**⚡ Quick Selection & Management**
- Multi-select photos with intuitive drag-to-select
- Delete multiple photos at once
- Smooth, lag-free performance even with thousands of photos
- Scroll position is preserved when navigating between screens

**💾 Backup & Restore**
- Export your tags to YAML files for backup
- Import tags from previous backups
- Never lose your photo organization

**🎨 Beautiful, Dark Theme**
- Modern dark interface that's easy on the eyes
- Clean, minimalist design focused on your photos
- Smooth animations and transitions

**🔍 Powerful Search**
- Filter photos by any combination of tags
- See photo counts for filtered results
- Quick access to all your tagged photos

### Perfect For
- Photographers organizing their work
- Travelers categorizing trip photos
- Parents organizing family memories
- Anyone who wants better photo organization

### Privacy First
- All data stays on your device
- No cloud sync, no data collection
- Your photos remain private and secure

Organize your photo library like never before. Download PhoTags today and take control of your memories.
